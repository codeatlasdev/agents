# CodeAtlas service discovery

Use the CodeAtlas public API or `/ask` to identify a suitable capability.

- `produto-digital`: tailored web/mobile software, APIs, e-commerce, and MVP delivery.
- `zelvo-ai`: AI-assisted customer service, lead qualification, WhatsApp, CRM, and support workflows.
- `consultoria`: process mapping, roadmaps, automation, integrations, and team enablement.

Return the service name, stable slug, description, and canonical URL. Say clearly that final scope and timeline require a diagnostic conversation with CodeAtlas (`contato@codeatlas.com.br`). Never quote prices.
